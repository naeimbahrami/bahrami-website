About Bahrami AI Consulting

Independent validation expertise for companies building production AI

Our Mission

Bahrami AI Consulting provides independent validation and technical consulting services for companies building production AI systems. We specialize in helping organizations ensure their AI models are accurate, fair, and ready for real-world deployment.

Our firm was founded to address a critical gap: too many AI systems fail in production due to inadequate validation during development. We bridge this gap by providing the deep technical expertise and rigorous testing needed to de-risk AI deployments and accelerate time to market.

Our Approach

We combine deep technical expertise with real-world implementation experience, built on over a decade of developing and deploying production AI systems.

Technical Depth

Our team brings hands-on experience building and deploying AI systems across computer vision, natural language processing, and deep learning. We've developed the same types of systems our clients are validating.

Production Focus

Our validation protocols are informed by direct experience deploying AI at scale. We understand what works in production environments and what challenges arise post-deployment.

Independent Objectivity

As a specialized validation firm with no ties to AI platform vendors or product companies, we provide genuinely objective assessment that stakeholders trust.

Boutique Service

Our clients work directly with senior-level experts who understand both technical nuances and business requirements. This ensures faster turnarounds and validation protocols tailored to your specific needs.
