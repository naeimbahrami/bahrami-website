About AI Consulting

Independent validation expertise for medical AI companies

Our Mission

AI Consulting provides independent validation and technical consulting services for medical AI companies navigating the path to regulatory approval. We specialize in helping innovative healthcare technologies achieve FDA clearance through rigorous, objective validation and expert regulatory support.

Our firm was founded to address a critical gap in the medical AI ecosystem: too many promising technologies face regulatory delays or failures due to inadequate validation. We bridge this gap by providing the deep technical expertise and regulatory intelligence needed to de-risk FDA submissions and accelerate time to market.

Our Approach

We combine deep technical expertise with regulatory intelligence, built on real-world experience developing FDA-regulated medical AI systems.

Technical Depth

Our team brings hands-on experience building and deploying medical AI systems across computer vision, natural language processing, and deep learning. We've developed the same types of systems our clients are validating.

Regulatory Intelligence

Our validation protocols are informed by direct experience navigating FDA pathways for medical devices. We understand what regulators look for and how to structure documentation that accelerates approval.

Independent Objectivity

As a specialized validation firm with no ties to AI platform vendors or device manufacturers, we provide genuinely objective assessment that regulators and hospitals trust.

Boutique Service

Our clients work directly with senior-level experts who understand both technical nuances and regulatory requirements. This ensures faster turnarounds and validation protocols tailored to your specific technology.
