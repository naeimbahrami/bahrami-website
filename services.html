Our Services

Specialized validation and consulting services for medical AI companies

Bahrami AI Consulting offers specialized validation and technical consulting services designed to de-risk FDA submissions and accelerate regulatory approval for medical AI companies.

We provide two core service lines, each addressing critical challenges in bringing medical AI systems to market.

AI Model Validation & Testing

Independent third-party validation for medical AI/ML models preparing for regulatory submission or clinical deployment.

What's Included

Performance Validation

  • Statistical analysis of model accuracy, sensitivity, specificity
  • Performance across diverse patient populations and demographics
  • Edge case and failure mode identification
  • Comparison against clinical benchmarks and existing standards

Bias & Fairness Assessment

  • Demographic bias analysis (age, gender, ethnicity)
  • Performance equity evaluation
  • Fairness metric calculation and reporting
  • Recommendations for bias mitigation

Robustness Testing

  • Model performance under varying input conditions
  • Sensitivity to data quality variations
  • Generalization across different clinical settings
  • Stress testing and boundary condition analysis

Clinical Validation Support

  • Real-world deployment readiness assessment
  • Clinical workflow integration analysis
  • Interpretability and explainability evaluation
  • Safety and failure mode analysis

Deliverables

  • Comprehensive validation report with statistical analysis
  • Performance metrics documentation
  • Test dataset descriptions and methodology
  • Recommendations for model improvement
  • FDA-ready documentation package
  • Executive summary for stakeholders

Typical Timeline

  • Basic Validation: 2-3 weeks
  • Comprehensive: 4-6 weeks
  • Premium Package: 6-10 weeks

Timelines depend on model complexity and documentation requirements.

Synthetic Medical Imaging Data Generation

Custom synthetic medical imaging datasets to solve data scarcity, privacy challenges, and training data limitations.

What's Included

Custom Dataset Generation

  • Synthetic images matched to your specific clinical use case
  • Multiple imaging modalities (CT, MRI, X-ray, fluoroscopy)
  • Pathology-specific synthetic samples
  • Configurable patient demographics and characteristics

Data Augmentation Services

  • Expansion of existing limited datasets
  • Maintaining statistical properties of original data
  • Controlled variation generation
  • Rare case and edge case synthesis

Quality Validation

  • Statistical fidelity assessment
  • Clinical realism evaluation
  • Comparison with real imaging data
  • Quality metrics and validation report

Applications

  • Training data augmentation for rare conditions
  • Privacy-preserving model development
  • Algorithm testing without patient data
  • Regulatory submission demonstration datasets
  • Performance benchmarking across diverse scenarios

Deliverables

  • Synthetic imaging dataset (specified volume and format)
  • Quality validation report with statistical analysis
  • Dataset documentation and metadata
  • Usage guidelines and recommendations
  • Source code for pipeline (optional)

Typical Timeline

  • Small Dataset (1K images): 2-3 weeks
  • Medium (5K images): 4-5 weeks
  • Large (20K+ images): 6-8 weeks

Custom timelines available for specialized requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical validation project take?

Most validation projects are completed in 2-6 weeks depending on complexity. We provide detailed timelines during the proposal phase.

Do you sign NDAs and BAAs?

Yes. We routinely work under confidentiality agreements and Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) for HIPAA compliance.

Can you validate models that are still in development?

Yes. Early-stage validation can identify issues before they become expensive problems. We offer pilot validations for models in development.

What data do you need from us?

For validation: your model (or API access) and test datasets. For synthetic data: example images and specifications. We work with you to define exact requirements.

Do you work with international companies?

Yes. We serve medical AI companies globally and are familiar with FDA, CE Mark, and other regulatory pathways.

What if you find issues during validation?

We provide detailed findings and actionable recommendations. Many clients iterate on their models and return for re-validation.

Can you help with post-market surveillance?

Yes. We offer ongoing monitoring and validation services for deployed AI systems as part of post-market requirements.

What makes your validation different from in-house testing?

FDA increasingly requires independent third-party validation. Our objective assessment carries more weight with regulators and provides the unbiased analysis hospitals need before deployment.
