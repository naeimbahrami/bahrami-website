Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical validation project take?

Most validation projects are completed in 2-6 weeks depending on model complexity and testing scope. We provide detailed timelines during the proposal phase.

Do you sign NDAs and data agreements?

Yes. We routinely work under confidentiality agreements and appropriate data protection agreements. Data security and privacy are paramount.

Can you validate models that are still in development?

Yes. Early-stage validation can identify issues before they become expensive problems. We offer pilot validations for models in development.

What data do you need from us?

For validation: your model (or API access) and test datasets. For synthetic data: example data and specifications. We work with you to define exact requirements.

Do you work with international companies?

Yes. We serve companies globally across all major industries and are experienced with various regulatory and compliance requirements.

What if you find issues during validation?

We provide detailed findings and actionable recommendations. Many clients iterate on their models and return for re-validation before production deployment.

Can you help with ongoing monitoring?

Yes. We offer ongoing validation services and monitoring strategy for deployed AI systems as part of production maintenance.

What makes your validation different from internal testing?

Independent third-party validation provides objective assessment and often reveals issues internal teams miss. Our cross-industry experience brings fresh perspective.
